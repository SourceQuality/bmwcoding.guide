# G Series Overview

The G series is BMW's current generation platform, introduced starting in 2018. These cars use the MGU head unit system and are very customizable through coding.

## Head Unit Generations

Your head unit determines what coding options you have and what tool versions you need.

### MGU (ID6/ID7)
- Found in G20, G30, G05, G80, G82
- 10.25" or 12.3" center screen
- Works with E-Sys and BimmerCode

### MGU2 (ID8/ID9, iDrive 8+)
- Found in G70, G60, and newer LCI models
- 12.3" instrument cluster + 14.9" curved center screen
- BimmerCode support varies by model. E-Sys needs newer PSdZData files.

## Identifying Your Chassis

1. **VIN decoder** - characters 4 through 8 of your VIN encode the model
2. **Vehicle menu** - iDrive > Settings > General > Legal/Vehicle data
3. **Door jamb sticker** - lists the model code

## Common Modules

| Module | Abbreviation | What it controls |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| Head Unit | HU_MGU / HU_MGU2 | Infotainment, display settings |
| Body Domain Controller | BDC | Lighting, locking, comfort features |
| Instrument Cluster | KOMBI | Gauges, digital speedo |
| Advanced Driver Assistance | KAFAS2 | Camera-based driver assist |
| Seat Module | SITZM | Seat memory, comfort features |
