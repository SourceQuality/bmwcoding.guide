# G80 / G82 - M3 & M4 (2021+)

The G80 M3 and G82 M4 sit on the CLAR platform with the S58 twin-turbo inline-6. They share a lot of coding overlap with the G20/G22, but have some extra M-specific options.

## Quick Facts

- **Production:** 2021+
- **Engine:** S58 3.0L twin-turbo I6
- **Head unit:** MGU (ID7), MGU2 (ID8) on CS and later models
- **OBD port:** Driver's side footwell
- **Recommended tools:** BimmerCode (easy), E-Sys (full access), xHP (transmission)

## M-Specific Coding

### Drivetrain

| Mod | Module | Tool | Difficulty |
|-----|--------|------|------------|
| xDrive torque split (xDrive models) | VTG | E-Sys | Advanced |
| [xHP transmission flash](/docs/tools/xhp-flasher) | EGS | xHP | Medium |

### Display / M Mode

| Mod | Module | Tool | Difficulty |
|-----|--------|------|------------|
| [M displays and gauges](/docs/mods/driving-modes) | HU_MGU | BimmerCode | Easy |
| M Drive settings | HU_MGU | BimmerCode | Easy |
| [Digital speedometer in cluster](/docs/mods/digital-speedometer) | KOMBI | BimmerCode | Easy |

### Exterior / Comfort

| Mod | Module | Tool | Difficulty |
|-----|--------|------|------------|
| [Ambient lighting](/docs/mods/ambient-lighting) | BDC | BimmerCode | Easy |
| Fold mirrors on lock | BDC | BimmerCode | Easy |

## M3 Touring (G81) Notes

The G81 M3 Touring shares the G80's coding options. It also has a few extras like tailgate height adjustment through the BDC module.

## Competition vs. Standard

Both Competition and non-Competition models share most modules. Some default values differ between them, but coding changes generally apply to both.

## Tips

- xHP flashing is separate from FDL coding. Check the [xHP guide](/docs/tools/xhp-flasher) for details.
- Most BDC and HU coding from the G20/G22 carries over to the M3/M4 for non-M-specific changes.
