# G70 — 7 Series (2023+)

The G70 7 Series is BMW's flagship sedan featuring iDrive 8 with the curved display, Theatre Screen rear entertainment, and extensive luxury features.

## Quick Facts

- **Production:** 2023+
- **Head unit:** MGU2 (ID8/ID9) — curved display standard
- **Platform:** CLAR WE (Weiterentwicklung / further development)
- **OBD port:** Driver's side footwell
- **Recommended tool:** BimmerCode (limited support), E-Sys (requires latest PSdZData)

:::note
The G70 uses newer module firmware than earlier G-series cars. Tool support is still evolving. Always verify compatibility with your specific build date.
:::

## Important Notes

- The G70 uses **BDC2** (second generation Body Domain Controller) instead of BDC — module paths differ from G20/G30/G05
- Many luxury comfort features (automatic doors, crystal headlights) are option-dependent and require the physical hardware to be present
- The new Theatre Screen rear display has its own module (RSE2) with limited third-party tool support currently
- E-Sys PSdZData version **4.45.20+** is generally required for G70 coding

## Also Applies To

The G70 platform and its modules are shared with:
- **G60** — 5 Series (2024+)
- **i5** — electric 5 Series
- **i7** — electric 7 Series (same chassis, different drivetrain modules)

Most BDC2 and HU_MGU2 coding from the G70 applies to these models as well.
